Who I Am
Developer. Author. Creator.

I'm Irtaza Hussain — an independent Android developer and published author from Pakistan. I operate under two professional brands: IZ Developers on Google Play Console and IZ Publications on Amazon KDP.

My work lives at the intersection of technology and creativity. I build practical, well-designed Android applications that solve real everyday problems, and I write books ranging from freelance productivity guides to compelling fiction.

I work with cutting-edge AI-assisted development workflows — leveraging Gemini AI agent mode inside Android Studio — which lets me ship high-quality, polished products efficiently as a solo creator.

Whether it's an app on the Play Store or a book on Amazon — every product I release is built with care, intention, and a focus on real value.
